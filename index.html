Essay Writing

Advanced structures and academic expressions for C1 essays.

💡 Essay Tip

Use a balanced structure: introduction → arguments → counterarguments → conclusion. Add inversions and advanced connectors to sound more academic.

🚀 Advanced Structures

It is widely acknowledged that...
Está ampliamente reconocido que...
It is widely acknowledged that social media has transformed modern communication.
Formal Introduction
Not only does this affect..., but it also impacts...
No solo afecta..., sino que también impacta...
Not only does excessive technology use affect teenagers, but it also impacts their mental health.
C1 Inversion
The benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks.
Los beneficios superan claramente los inconvenientes.
Although remote work presents some disadvantages, the benefits clearly outweigh the drawbacks.
Balanced Opinion

Report Writing

Formal structures and recommendations for reports.

📊 Report Tip

Keep the tone objective and organised. Use headings, findings, and recommendations.

The aim of this report is to...
El objetivo de este informe es...
The aim of this report is to evaluate the effectiveness of online learning tools.
Formal Opening
It was found that...
Se descubrió que...
It was found that most students preferred interactive activities.
Findings

Article Writing

Dynamic and engaging expressions for articles.

📰 Article Tip

Use rhetorical questions and engaging openings to catch the reader’s attention.

Have you ever wondered why...?
¿Alguna vez te has preguntado por qué...?
Have you ever wondered why people spend so much time on social media?
Catchy Opening
Like it or not,...
Nos guste o no...
Like it or not, artificial intelligence is becoming part of our daily lives.
Natural Style

Formal Email

Useful formal expressions for professional emails.

📩 Email Tip

Be polite, concise, and clear. Use formal openings and closings.

I am writing to inquire about...
Le escribo para preguntar sobre...
I am writing to inquire about the possibility of attending the course online.
Formal Request
I look forward to hearing from you.
Quedo a la espera de su respuesta.
I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.
Formal Closing

Complaint Letter

Polite but firm language for complaints.

⚠ Complaint Tip

Stay formal and respectful even when expressing dissatisfaction.

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with...
Le escribo para expresar mi descontento con...
I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with the service provided.
Formal Complaint

Mediation

Reformulating and summarising information effectively.

🤝 Mediation Tip

Avoid translating literally. Reformulate ideas naturally and clearly.

The text explains that...
El texto explica que...
The text explains that sustainable habits can reduce environmental impact.
Summarising
In other words,...
En otras palabras...
In other words, technology should be used responsibly.
Reformulation
